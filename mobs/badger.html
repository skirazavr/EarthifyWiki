Badger

The Badger is a passive animal added by Earthify.

Badgers dig dirt-like blocks and occasionally uncover useful items.

Behavior

Badgers can dig Grass Blocks, Dirt, Coarse Dirt, Podzol and Rooted Dirt. After digging, they may uncover various natural resources and treasures. Could be bred with EarthifyItemTags. BADGER_FOOD items.
