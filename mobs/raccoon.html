Raccoon

The Raccoon is a passive woodland animal added by Earthify. It is a curious creature that spends its days exploring forests, begging players for food, and taking long naps under the shade of trees.

Unlike most passive mobs, raccoons have several unique behaviors. They can stand on their hind legs while begging, fall asleep during the day, and react to nearby players with lifelike animations.

Behavior

Raccoons naturally wander through forests and plains. They avoid sleeping during rain and will immediately wake up if attacked or disturbed by nearby players. When they feel safe, especially beneath trees or other shelters, they may decide to lie down and sleep for 30–90 seconds before continuing their daily routine.

If a player approaches while holding an item from ModItemTags.RACCOON_FOOD, the raccoon becomes interested and walks toward them. Once close enough, it stands on its hind legs and begins begging. During this animation it occasionally makes cute begging sounds.

Baby raccoons follow their parents until they become adults. When damaged, raccoons panic, stop sleeping immediately, and remain alert for a while before resting again.
