Viler Witch

The Viler Witch is a hostile illager mob added by Earthify.

Unlike a normal witch, the Viler Witch throws lingering potions, creating dangerous clouds that affect multiple targets at once. It can also support allied raiders during raids with beneficial potions.

Spawning

Viler Witches can appear during raids. They begin spawning from later raid waves and become more common as the raid progresses.

Combat

  • Throws Lingering Potions.
  • Uses Poison against healthy enemies.
  • Uses Slowness against distant targets.
  • May use Weakness against nearby targets.
  • Uses Harming when no other potion is more suitable.
  • Can heal or regenerate allied raiders.
