Furnace Golem

The Furnace Golem is a powerful golem added by Earthify.

Created from an Iron Golem, the Furnace Golem serves as a stronger guardian capable of using intense heat against hostile creatures. When enemies approach, its furnace ignites and burns nearby targets.

Behavior

Furnace Golems patrol villages and attack hostile mobs. They will never attack players and completely ignore Creepers.

Furnace Mode

When a hostile mob gets too close, the Furnace Golem can activate its furnace. During this state, nearby enemies take damage and are set on fire.

  • Burns nearby hostile mobs.
  • Sets enemies on fire for several seconds.
  • Remains active for a limited duration.
  • Requires a cooldown before activating again.

Combat

Furnace Golems deal heavy melee damage and launch enemies into the air. Combined with their burning aura, they are extremely effective against large groups of monsters.

Repairing

Furnace Golems can be repaired using Smooth Stone. Using Smooth Stone restores 20 health points.

Spawning

When an Iron Golem spawns, there is a 50% chance that it will be replaced by a Furnace Golem instead.

Furnace Golems retain the position and orientation of the original Iron Golem and immediately begin acting as village guardians.
