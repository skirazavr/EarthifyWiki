Bone Spider

The Bone Spider is a powerful undead spider added by Earthify.

Unlike ordinary spiders, Bone Spiders possess thick skeletal armor and can launch Bone Shards at distant targets.

Behavior

  • Hostile towards players and iron golems.
  • Uses both melee and ranged attacks.
  • Launches Bone Shards that inflict Weakness.
  • Becomes passive in bright light.
  • Immune to freezing damage.
  • Harmed by healing effects and healed by harming effects.

Combat

Bone Spiders attack nearby enemies with melee attacks and throw Bone Shards at distant targets. Bone Shards apply Weakness for 5 seconds.
