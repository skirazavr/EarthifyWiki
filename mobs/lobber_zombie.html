Lobber Zombie

The Lobber Zombie is a hostile undead mob added by Earthify.

Unlike normal zombies, Lobber Zombies can throw Harder Flesh projectiles at distant targets before engaging in melee combat.

Behavior

Lobber Zombies attack players, villagers, iron golems and baby turtles. They can switch between ranged and melee combat depending on the distance to their target.

  • Throws Harder Flesh projectiles.
  • Attacks in melee at close range.
  • Can convert into a Drowned underwater.
  • May summon zombie reinforcements.

Combat

When its target is far away, a Lobber Zombie throws Harder Flesh. Once the target gets close enough, it attacks like a normal zombie.
