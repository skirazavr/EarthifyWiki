Wooly Cow

The Wooly Cow is a shaggy cow variant added by Earthify.

Covered in a thick coat of wool, Wooly Cows combine traits of both cows and sheep. Their wool can be harvested and naturally regrows over time.

Behavior

  • Passive mob.
  • Can be bred using Wheat.
  • Can be sheared.
  • Regrows wool after grazing.
  • Baby Wooly Cows grow faster after eating grass.

Shearing

Adult Wooly Cows can be sheared with Shears.

Once sheared, they lose their wool coat until it regrows.

Shearing yields Brown Wool.

Brown Wool 1–3 (when sheared)

Wool Regrowth

Wooly Cows regrow their wool by eating grass, similarly to sheep.

After grazing, a sheared Wooly Cow becomes fully woolly again.

Breeding

Two Wooly Cows can breed to produce a baby Wooly Cow.

Drops

Leather 0–2
Raw Beef 1–3
Cooked Beef 1–3 (if killed while on fire)
