Cluckshroom

The Cluckshroom is a mushroom-covered chicken added by Earthify.

Similar to Mooshrooms, Cluckshrooms come in both red and brown variants and can be sheared to transform into a normal Chicken.

Spawning

Cluckshrooms naturally spawn on Mycelium in bright conditions.

Variants

  • Red Cluckshroom
  • Brown Cluckshroom

Being struck by lightning causes a Cluckshroom to switch between its red and brown variants.

Breeding

Cluckshrooms can breed with each other.

When two Cluckshrooms of the same type breed, there is a 1 in 1024 chance for the offspring to be the opposite variant.

Shearing

Adult Cluckshrooms can be sheared.

  • Transforms into a normal Chicken.
  • Drops 5 mushrooms matching its variant.
  • Creates an explosion particle effect.

Special Eggs

Instead of laying normal eggs, Cluckshrooms periodically produce special mushroom-themed loot.
