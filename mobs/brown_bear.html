Brown Bear

The Brown Bear is a neutral animal added by Earthify. It is a powerful creature that normally remains peaceful, but becomes aggressive when provoked or when a nearby bear cub is present.

Brown Bears are capable of fishing, scratching themselves, following their parents as babies, breeding, swimming, and defending their young. Their behavior is designed around their natural activities rather than constant aggression. They spawn only in forests

Behavior

Brown Bears are neutral by default. They do not automatically attack nearby players simply because a player is present. A bear can become angry after being attacked, and its anger can persist for a limited period of time.

Adult Brown Bears become especially protective when a baby Brown Bear is nearby. An adult can target nearby players while protecting a cub.

Baby Brown Bears do not directly attack players. If a baby is hurt, however, it alerts nearby adult Brown Bears, causing the adults to defend the cub against its attacker.

Protecting Cubs

When a baby Brown Bear is attacked, it alerts nearby adult Brown Bears. Only adult bears respond to this alert, allowing the family group to defend the young bear.

Following Parents

Baby Brown Bears follow their parents while they are young. Once they mature, they no longer use the parent-following behavior.

Scratching

Brown Bears occasionally stop to scratch themselves. During the scratching animation, the bear stops moving and temporarily prevents other movement goals from controlling it.

Scratching occurs randomly and has a cooldown before the behavior can happen again.

Fishing

Brown Bears can fish while standing in water. When a bear begins fishing, it stops moving and waits before catching a fish.

A successful catch produces one of three fish: Salmon, Cod, or Tropical Fish.

Fishing also creates splash particles and plays a fishing splash sound. After fishing, the bear enters a cooldown before it can fish again.

Swimming

Brown Bears are comfortable in water and have reduced water slowdown. Their movement AI also specifically values positions above water, allowing them to naturally seek out watery areas when fishing is available.

Breeding

Brown Bears are breedable animals. Their food is determined by the items accepted by the bear's food tag.

Combat

Adult Brown Bears can attack living targets using their melee attack. Their attack behavior has a dedicated attack goal and changes their standing state while preparing an attack.

Brown Bears have a persistent anger system. Once angered, the bear remembers its target for a period of time instead of immediately becoming neutral again.

Drops

Brown Bears drop Raw Bear Meat when killed. The amount dropped is affected by the Looting enchantment.

If the bear is killed while on fire, the meat is automatically converted into Cooked Bear Meat.
