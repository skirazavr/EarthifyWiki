Owl

The Owl is a passive animal added by Earthify. It is a flying mob that can perch on tree branches and wander between trees.

Owls can be bred using EarthifyItemTags. OWL_FOOD items.

Behavior

Owls are flying animals that use flying navigation and can move freely through the air.

They can perch on leaves and logs when looking for a suitable place to rest. Their wandering behavior allows them to search for nearby trees and land on available branches.

When in water, Owls attempt to find a position on land instead of continuing to wander through the water.

Owls are passive and do not attack other entities. When frightened, they can panic and attempt to escape.

Spawning

Owls can spawn under the standard animal spawning conditions. They can also spawn when the block at their spawning position is one of the supported leaf blocks or air.

Breeding

Owls can be bred using items from the EarthifyItemTags. OWL_FOOD item tag.

Baby Owls follow their parents and grow into adult Owls over time.
