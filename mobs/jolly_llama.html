Jolly Llama

The Jolly Llama is a festive llama variant added by Earthify.

Jolly Llamas behave similarly to normal llamas but feature a unique holiday appearance.

Behavior

Jolly Llamas are passive animals that can be bred with other Jolly Llamas.

  • Passive mob.
  • Can form caravans.
  • Can be equipped with carpets.
  • Can be bred with other Jolly Llamas.

Breeding

Two Jolly Llamas can breed to produce a baby Jolly Llama.
