Penguin

The Penguin is a passive aquatic animal added by Earthify. It lives along frozen coastlines, icy oceans and snow-covered shores where it spends most of its life swimming, hunting fish and sliding across ice.

Penguins are highly adapted to aquatic environments. Although they can walk on land, they move much faster in water and frequently dive beneath the surface in search of food.

Behavior

Penguins naturally alternate between walking, swimming, floating, and sliding across ice. When standing on Ice, Packed Ice or Blue Ice, they occasionally perform a belly slide, allowing them to move quickly across frozen terrain.

Unlike most passive animals, penguins actively hunt schools of fish. Adult penguins attack nearby schooling fish, catch them, and may carry their prey in their beak before eating it.

Penguins also search for dropped food items on the ground. If they find food from ModItemTags.PENGUIN_FOOD, they pick it up and eventually eat it. If they accidentally pick up something inedible, they spit it back onto the ground.

Penguins are excellent swimmers and automatically search for nearby water if they become stranded on land. They can stay underwater for a long time thanks to their increased air supply.

Players can breed penguins using ModItemTags.PENGUIN_FOOD. Baby penguins follow their parents until they become adults.

Spawning

Penguins naturally spawn on Ice, Packed Ice, Blue Ice and in shallow water. They prefer frozen oceans and snowy environments where water and ice are abundant.
