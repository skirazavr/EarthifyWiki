Dyed Cat

The Dyed Cat is a colorful cat variant added by Earthify.

Unlike ordinary cats, Dyed Cats feature vibrant fur colors and can only be found in Swamp biomes.

Spawning

Dyed Cats naturally spawn in Swamps.

They do not generate in villages or other cat spawning locations outside swamp biomes.

Behavior

Dyed Cats behave similarly to regular cats.

  • Passive mob.
  • Can be tamed by players.
  • Scares away Creepers.
  • Can breed with other Dyed Cats.

Breeding

Two Dyed Cats can breed to produce a baby Dyed Cat.

Dyed Cats cannot breed with ordinary Cats.

Uses

Dyed Cats make excellent companions and decorative pets thanks to their unique appearance.
