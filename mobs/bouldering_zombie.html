Bouldering Zombie

The Bouldering Zombie is a hostile undead mob added by Earthify.

Unlike ordinary zombies, Bouldering Zombies are capable of climbing walls and rough terrain, making them particularly dangerous in caves and mountainous regions.

Behavior

  • Can climb vertical walls.
  • Converts into a Drowned underwater.
  • Has increased armor and knockback resistance.
  • May call zombie reinforcements.

Spawning

Bouldering Zombies spawn underground below Y=0 and in mountain biomes under low light conditions.

Combat

Thanks to their climbing ability, Bouldering Zombies can pursue targets over obstacles that would normally stop other zombies.
