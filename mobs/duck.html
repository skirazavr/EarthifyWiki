Duck

The Duck is a passive waterfowl added by Earthify.

Ducks can swim, breed, and lay different colored duck eggs depending on their variant.

Variants

Ducks have several biome-based variants.

  • Temperate Duck
  • Cold Duck
  • Warm Duck

Behavior

  • Can swim in water.
  • Lays duck eggs periodically.
  • Can breed using EarthifyItemTags. DUCK_FOOD items.
  • Baby ducks are smaller than adult ducks.

Eggs

Adult ducks periodically lay eggs when not in water.

Green Duck Egg Temperate Variant
White Duck Egg Cold Variant
Cream Duck Egg Warm Variant

Drops

Duck Meat 1
Cooked Duck Meat 1 (if killed while on fire)
