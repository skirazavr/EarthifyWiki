Moolip

The Moolip is a flower-covered cow added by Earthify.

Similar to the Moobloom, Moolips grow Pink Daisies on their backs and can provide flowers and Suspicious Stew through interaction.

Behavior

  • Passive mob.
  • Can breed with other Moolips.
  • Can be sheared.
  • Produces Pink Daisies.
  • Can create Suspicious Stew.

Pink Daisies

Using Bone Meal on an adult Moolip produces a Pink Daisy.

Moolips are permanently covered in Pink Daisies and can generate additional flowers through player interaction.

Suspicious Stew

Using a Bowl on an adult Moolip creates Suspicious Stew.

The stew inherits the effects associated with Pink Daisies.

Shearing

Adult Moolips can be sheared.

  • Transforms into a normal Cow.
  • Drops 4 Pink Daisies.
  • Creates an explosion particle effect.

Drops

Leather 0–2
Raw Beef 1–3
Cooked Beef 1–3 (if killed while on fire)
Pink Daisy 4 (when sheared)
