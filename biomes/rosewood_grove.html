Rosewood Grove

Rosewood Grove is a forest biome added by Earthify.

Covered in towering Rosewood trees, colorful flowers and berry bushes, Rosewood Grove is one of the most vibrant biomes in Earthify. It is home to several unique creatures and naturally generated structures.
