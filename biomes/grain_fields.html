Grain Fields

Grain Fields is an agricultural biome added by Earthify.

Wide open fields of grain stretch across the landscape, accompanied by scattered fir trees, and barley. The biome is designed around the feeling of natural farmland gradually blending into the surrounding forest.

Barley is the defining feature of Grain Fields and can be found growing naturally throughout the biome.

Features

  • Natural Barley Fields
  • Fir Trees

Vegetation

  • Barley
  • Fir Tree

Terrain

Grain Fields consists primarily of open terrain suitable for large areas of naturally growing grain. Fir trees are scattered throughout the landscape, breaking up the open fields and creating a transition between farmland and forest.

Underground features and natural ore generation remain similar to other overworld biomes, while springs and surface features contribute to the natural appearance of the terrain.

Climate

Grain Fields has a mild and humid climate with frequent precipitation. Its warm temperature and relatively high rainfall create favorable conditions for dense vegetation and naturally occurring grain.
