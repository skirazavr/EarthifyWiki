Bestiary

The Bestiary is a book added by Earthify. It provides information about animals and helps players find them in the world.

Obtaining

Players receive a Bestiary automatically the first time they join a world. It is only granted once per player.

Usage

When used, it displays a list of all animals that can appear in the Bestiary. Animals the player has already discovered are shown with their information, while undiscovered animals remain hidden until they are encountered. The contents of the Bestiary update dynamically as new animals are found, gradually expanding the available entries.

Customization

The list of animals included in the Bestiary is configurable through the EarthifyEntityTypeTags. BESTIARY entity type tag. Any entity assigned to this tag will automatically appear in the Bestiary, making it easy for addons to extend its contents.
