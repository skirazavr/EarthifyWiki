Small Stable

Small Stable is a structure added by Earthify.

Built primarily from Rosewood, Small Stables are rustic buildings commonly found in Rosewood Grove biome. These structures serve as shelters for livestock but are often occupied by hostile illagers.

Generation

Small Stables generate naturally in Rosewood Grove biomes.

The structure is primarily constructed from Rosewood Planks, Rosewood Logs and other Rosewood building materials.

The structure can appear in biomes belonging to the EarthifyBiomeTags.HAS_SMALL_STABLE biome tag.

Inhabitants

Small Stables override normal monster spawning inside the structure.

Illusioner 1–2
Pillager 1–4
Ravager 1

Features

  • Contains illager mobs.
  • Can spawn Ravagers.
  • Terrain-adaptive generation.

Loot

Loot varies depending on the generated structure pieces.
